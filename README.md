## Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive collection of **Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)** programming questions that I encountered during my technical interview preparation. These are the **most commonly asked questions by recruiters** and tech companies during coding interviews.

Whether you're preparing for interviews at FAANG companies or any tech organization, this resource is designed to help you master the essential DSA concepts and problem-solving patterns.

---

## 🎓 Why Interview Prep is Different

**Important Reality Check:** The work you do in a company is very different from what you'll face in coding interviews!

In your **regular job**, you typically work with:
- Existing codebases and frameworks
- Pre-built libraries and APIs
- Well-defined business requirements
- Collaborative environments with code reviews

In **coding interviews**, you'll encounter:
- **Algorithm-heavy problems** you may never use in actual work
- **Data structure optimization** challenges
- **Edge cases** and tricky scenarios
- **Optimization problems** (time & space complexity)
- **Problems from scratch** without external libraries

**This repository focuses specifically on interview-style questions** - the kind that test your problem-solving fundamentals, algorithmic thinking, and DSA knowledge. These are the gatekeepers that companies use to evaluate your coding ability!
